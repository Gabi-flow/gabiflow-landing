Set up your store

In a few minutes, step by step. Just follow the screens.

1

Sign in

Open GabiFlow and tap "Create account".

2

Account

Enter your email and a password. You'll get an activation link by email — click it.

3

Create your store

Enter a name & phone, and choose a category (e.g. Hair Salon).

4

Store type

Confirm what type of store you run.

5

Services

Pick ready-made services with duration & price — or add your own.

6

Opening hours

Set when your store is open.

7

Team

Add your team members (if you have staff).

8

Done!

Your store is ready — start taking appointments!
